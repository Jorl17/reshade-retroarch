Put RetroArch slang shaders in this folder.

The easiest start is the libretro collection:
  https://github.com/libretro/slang-shaders  (Code -> Download ZIP)
Extract it here, so you get retroarch-shaders\slang-shaders\...

Preset packs (CyberLab, Mega Bezel, ...) go here too, laid out the way the pack
expects to sit in RetroArch's shaders_slang folder.

If RetroArch is installed, its shaders are found automatically and this folder
can stay empty.

Then start the game, press Home, open the "RetroArch Shaders" tab, pick a preset
and press "Use for this ReShade preset".
